# Playtest notes — 2026-10-28

Five crossings on snag.astounding.games (desktop Chrome, about 1000×1000 viewport).
Opponents in order: Lenny King, Ignatz, Kitty Sawyer, Eliza Bathory, Finlay MacTavish.
Finished round 5 first in the standings at $79.

## Bugs and inconsistencies

### 1. The table shifts when a snag is at stake
When a board snag came up on the Turn (round 5, Runaway Mule), the line
"At stake: the Runaway Mule" appeared under the board and the whole table moved
up about 15px: the name, portrait, cards and the street header. This goes against "fixed rows that never shift".

- Where: `src/ui/DrawTable.svelte:806`,
  `{#if stakes.length}<p class="stakes">…</p>{/if}`.
- Fix: always render the `<p class="stakes">` element and leave it empty (or
  use `visibility: hidden`) when there is nothing at stake, so its height is
  always reserved. `Table.svelte:156` (the slots table) already does this with a
  `table.noStakes` fallback.

### 2. "At stake" stays on the standings screen
After I folded in round 5, "At stake: the Runaway Mule" was still showing under
the standings. After a fold the snag goes back into the deck, so nothing is at
stake any more.

- Fix: hide the line when `standing` is true (or once `result` is set). With
  fix 1, that means emptying the reserved row, not removing it.

### 3. A snag in the opponent's fan flips back and forth
In round 3, Kitty fished a snag on her Turn swap. On the River it showed
face-up in her fan (the "Fog Bank" art). When the showdown started it was
face-down among her hidden cards, and it was turned face-up again during the reveal.

- Fix: owned snags are public information, so keep them face-up all the time.
  If the reveal should still flip played snags ("the stakes go up!"), mark the
  played ones some other way (lift them, outline them) rather than hiding
  a card the player has already seen.

### 4. Skip goes past the verdict
In round 4, Skip during the reveal went straight to the standings. I never saw
"You win!" or which cards the opponent held back.

- Fix: make Skip go to the verdict state (all cards turned, verdict on the
  white button), and let Next take you on to the standings. The doc's wording is
  "Skip (to the result)".

### 5. "Still open: … % against you" never appeared
While Lenny's cards were turning over in round 1 (9♦ shown, then 10♥ and J♥ still to come,
straight live), the callout only read "High card, King". The design doc says it
shows "Still open: Three of a kind · 27% against you", and `show.possible`
("Still possible: {hands}!") exists in en.ts.

- Fix: check the condition that shows the teaser. It may only fire for certain
  categories, or after a certain card. If it is meant to show only sometimes,
  update the doc.

### 6. A disabled Fold button still shows a price on the River
On the River the Fold button is disabled (correct, `canFold` needs
`street < 2`) but still reads "Fold · $4". It looks like an option that has been
greyed out, rather than one that doesn't exist.

- Fix: on the River, show the label without the price, or hide the button
  and let Play hand span both columns. The doc rule ("face the music") can
  stay as it is.

### 7. Placeholder portraits
Every opponent is shown as a letter in a circle. This is known, I assume, but it's
the biggest visual gap next to the finished cards and coins.

## Doc cleanup (docs/design.md)

- The snag-capacity table leaves out pair (3) and high card (4).
- "money for a win", "Loser pays" and "End of round" still describe fixed payouts
  and the end-of-round drain from the slots era. In the pot game, upkeep is
  paid in the ante.
- The Hand ranking section talks about slots and "exactly 2 of your 3 slots".
- The title card text says "Steer public opinion. Avoid ruin…". The live title shows
  "Sharp Cards, Deep Water, Stinking Hides".
- "Months" and "crossings" are both used for the same thing (and `shuffledJokerEveryMonths`
  in the config).
- The snag count is 10 in the config. In conversation it was 8.

## Design observations (not bugs)

- **Snags arrive slowly.** In five crossings I never owned one. The only snag
  pressure came from one fished card (Kitty) and one board snag. In a first
  session, the core mechanic hardly appears. Options: put a snag into the deck
  from crossing 1, or give the new player a snag at the start (a reason to
  learn dumping right away).
- **Two pair vs trips:** with snags in hand, two pair carries 1 snag and trips
  carry 2. Trips also rank higher, so two pair is never the better dump. The sim shows two pair as 38% of
  hands, so this comes up often.
- **Game length:** the sim's winning games run 45–54 crossings. That's long for
  a web session. Ante growth and upkeep are the levers.

## What worked well

- Coin purses and the coin pot: I always knew what I'd paid without seeing a number.
- Prices on every button ("Swap 2 · $3"): every choice is a money choice.
- The hand call plus table talk on the River ("Two pair, Aces and Eights / The
  Dead Man's Hand", "Route 66", "Dimes over Kicks").
- Face-up discards: standing pat was the strongest read at the table.
- The board snag: "At stake" made a fold on a weak draw an easy, satisfying call.
