## Bonus tracks:

All of these are 100% public domain in Norway/Europe right now (and 1924–1925 sides are also already public domain in the U.S.).
• Fate Marable's Society Syncopators — "Frankie and Johnny" (March 16, 1924 — OKeh 40113)
	• Composer: Traditional / Leighton Brothers (Bert Leighton died 1964, Frank Leighton died 1927; first published 1912—public domain in the U.S., though if strictly following EU life+70 for the 1912 Leighton arrangement, Bert Leighton's 1964 death runs until 2035; the underlying traditional 1890s melody/ballad itself is public domain everywhere).
