# The voyage: itinerary

Steamboat to St. Louis, every stop and every encounter in order, for maps. River miles are
the Upper Mississippi's, counted up from the mouth of the Ohio at Cairo; they and the
coordinates are approximate (from memory, to verify). Encounters sit somewhere on the
stretch they're listed under; a map can place them anywhere plausible along it.
