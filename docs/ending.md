# The ending (Nemo's, from slumberland)

**St. Louis, the Fair.** Pretty Patty drives off to confetti and fanfare: a great success.
The journey that got her there goes entirely uncelebrated. The *Best Intentions* lists at
the dock, paddles broken, a chimney snapped off. The crew stands on the dock watching
Patty's parade go, frayed and properly beaten. Gaylord too: he took the seven ledges with
the rest of them.

- **Gus:** "So what's next, boss?"
- The player: **The End.** The crew won't have it.
- **Willie:** "No boss, this ain't the end. I'll go ask around, see if I can find us some
  jobs. We'll need some cash to fix her up."
- **Gus:** "Aye, much to do. I'll get started on the crankshaft, it took a beating. Lars,
  you see about the chimney."
- **Lars:** ♪ ♫
- **The cook:** a stand on the boardwalk: biscuits, sweet buns, and iced tea (the drink
  of the 1904 Fair; the mint julep belongs to Kentucky). Her line to be written.
- They all look at **Gaylord.** He straightens his hat and jacket, fixes his necktie.
  "I…" "Ahem. I'll go up to the World's Fair's International High Rollers table. Try to
  win us some money to fix the engine up. Captain, are you coming?"

## The credits reel

A looping animation for as long as anyone cares to watch, while the credits roll:

- Things keep falling off the boat onto **Gaylord** as he comes back aboard. He shakes his
  fist at **Lars** on the roof, who shrugs and sings his notes. Gaylord stomps off, then
  comes back a minute later and it happens again.
- **Willie** carries planks and turns with them, always narrowly missing **the cook**, who
  is always back to pick something up, and always hitting **Gus**. Gus falls over, chases
  Willie off screen, goes back inside, comes out with planks himself, and round it goes.

**Credits:** the two of us; sources and inspiration: the maps (1890 Mississippi River
Commission survey via USGS/UMESC, USGS historical topos), the public-domain songs
("Steamboat Bill", 1910, and "Kansas City Shuffle", Bennie Moten's Kansas City Orchestra, 1926), *Steamboat
Willie* (Walt Disney, Ub Iwerks), *Krazy Kat* and Ignatz (George Herriman), and anyone
else who deserves it.

Then the menu: League games, and maybe one day multiplayer.
